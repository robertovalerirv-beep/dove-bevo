## Data safety / App Privacy worksheet

“Candidate” means a conservative starting point for the actual store form. Apple's [App Privacy definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) exclude wholly on-device data and assess retained off-device app/partner data. Google's [Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) cover off-device transmission, including pseudonymous data; collection, sharing and optionality are separate decisions. Google defines exceptions for some service-provider and user-initiated transfers. Do not use an exception to sharing as an exemption from collection.

## English reviewer instructions — draft

Use only after the production candidate and a populated review area are verified. Add the actual area and release-specific facts before submitting. Do not paste preview fixture access as production access.

> Dove bevo is an Italian-language map of venues and community-reported drink prices. Consumer browsing does not require an account. Search for the populated review area supplied with this release, choose a drink, then open a venue to see observation dates and confirmations. Location is optional; “Vicino a me” requests foreground permission, and city/name search remains available if permission is denied.
>
> To contribute, choose “Aggiungi prezzo” and a venue/drink. The submission action links and accepts the contribution rules. The first protected action can open a Cloudflare anti-bot page in the browser and return to the app; there are no username/password credentials. New venue proposals are checked before public publication. Open a bar and choose “Segnala un errore” to report incorrect or offensive content. Content reports are handled by Roberto at dovebevo@gmail.com.
>
> “Sponsorizzato” identifies paid venue placement. Community prices are independent of that placement. The app provides information; it has no alcohol ordering, delivery or payment feature. It is intended for adults.

Before submission, attach the verified populated area and real device evidence, resolve the blocking gate above, and make this note match the final controls. Do not describe a future blocking feature as implemented.
