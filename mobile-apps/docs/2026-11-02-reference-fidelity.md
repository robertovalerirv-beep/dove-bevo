# Reference-led mobile presentation update

The owner's six-screen image is the visual target for the existing phone apps: compact map controls and price pins, photographic venue/price detail, clear price entry, photographic discovery cards, a shareable price image, and a native information screen.

This is an app-only presentation change. The existing website source, public data, backend and price contracts are preserved. The newer Home, Mappa, Preferiti, Salva and Consigliati bottom controls remain; Condividi and Ricarica remain in the overflow. The original orange action colour is retained.

## Real imagery and real information

Eight original generic drink photographs cover the nine existing drink categories. Gin lemon and gin tonic share a lemon-garnished gin image. The existing Spritz product photograph remains in the price form. A separate original community illustration appears in the native information view. The photographic category scenes are illustrative, labelled accordingly, and never claim to be a photograph of the selected business. No invented venue photograph or customer contribution is introduced.

The compact venue view preserves its actual name, address, drink prices, price date, confirmations, reports and public contribution actions. European rows additionally preserve their original currency, serving, time band and menu-source evidence. Unsupported photo upload, account history, paid-date selection and price sentiment voting are not presented as working controls.

## Native image sharing

When a single visible featured price belongs to the current trusted venue document, the app can create a local branded price card and show a preview before opening the system share sheet. The read-only document marker carries the canonical venue key and visible name, category, amount in minor units, currency, exact serving, happy-hour flag, provenance, confirmations, price tier and city. It does not publish or alter a price.

Native readers reject hidden, nonmodal, obscured, malformed, stale or mismatched records, and retain the canonical mobile URL share fallback. A menu-check date remains a source-check date; it is not presented as the date a customer paid. Long source conditions wrap in the card. Android grants temporary read access only to a generated image in the private price-share cache through its non-exported app FileProvider. iOS shares a local image and URL without adding photo-library access.

## Verification boundary

Source checks and fixture captures do not establish a public launch or real-iPhone acceptance. The integrated source must pass both native build gates, and the real rendered states must be compared against the reference. Release and provider state are recorded separately in the release evidence.
