## Prices and fee scope

- The manifest records **55 printed, named drink/serving offers**: eight `Birra alla Spina` size/price combinations, 24 cocktails (eight signature and 16 classic), 11 ciders, three wine glasses, three fixed-price spirits, five soft drinks and one 50 cl water. Multi-option menu labels such as `London / Moscow Mule` and `Coca Cola / Coca Zero` remain grouped exactly as printed with their single shared price; they are not multiplied into invented variants. These are owner-menu offers, not observed customer receipts.
- Completeness check against the pinned live HTML: its 40 `drink-row` elements comprise 39 fixed-price rows matched to the manifest and one `Whisky` price range `5–16` that cannot be turned into an exact price. Its eight signature cocktail rows and all eight size-specific prices in the five `Birra alla Spina` cards are matched too: **39 + 8 + 8 = 55**. The separate `Birre della Settimana` section explicitly rotates with availability, so its named weekly beers are held rather than presented as generally available. `Champagne Bollinger` is marked “Presto in Arrivo” without a price and is excluded. Food and unpriced items are excluded.
- The draft-beer header prints `Piccola 25cl / Media 40cl`. The size-specific rows preserve those labels; `250` and `400` ml are exact unit conversions. No serving volume is assigned to cocktails or wine glasses beyond the printed `Calice` label. The menu is not a promise that a particular beer remains on tap; the protected audit must recheck availability and digest.
- Under `Informazioni extra`, the menu prints `Coperto +2`. The manifest records a separate EUR 2 fixed fee with **unspecified basis and unknown applicability** because the owner does not say whether it is per person or applies to drinks-only orders. `documented_partial` does not claim that every fee is known. The printed drink amount is never increased silently.
- Only factual drink names, amounts and serving labels are transcribed. No menu descriptions, images, Google review counts, inferred visits, confirmations, currency conversions or public/customer writes are included.
