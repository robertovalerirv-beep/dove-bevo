1. Purpose and audience
Dove Bevo helps adults find bars and compare drink prices, save favourite venues privately, and view aggregate drink recommendations. It provides venue information; it does not sell, deliver or process payment for alcohol.

2. Access and demonstration
No account, sign-in or demo credentials are required. On first launch, read the rules/privacy information and confirm that you are 18 or older. Location is optional: deny permission and search for a city to browse. Open a venue to view prices and recommendations, save it to Favourites, and open Scopri to explore an area. Internet access is required for maps and current server data. Price-entry, venue-proposal and reporting forms can be inspected and cancelled without submitting public content.

3. Contributions and safety
Contributions are structured numeric prices, price confirmations and drink-category votes. Venue proposals include a name, type and map location. Pending proposals are excluded from normal venue browsing, but may appear labelled as unconfirmed in the price-entry venue picker. Report controls accept reasons including incorrect information and offensive content. Reports are handled by the operator; they do not guarantee immediate removal. The app has no public author profiles, direct messages, comments, review text or photo uploads. We do not claim a consumer user-blocking feature. Support and content complaints: dovebevo@gmail.com.

4. External services
The app uses the first-party HTTPS service at www.dovebevo.com on Cloudflare, Cloudflare Turnstile for contribution protection, and OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap mapping. Directions open Apple Maps after the user chooses directions. Sharing and email open system interfaces only after the user's action. There is no native advertising or tracking SDK.

5. Regional differences
Venue coverage varies by area: Italy and selected European cities are available, including Paris, London and Madrid. Available menu prices preserve their stated currency and serving size: GBP for the UK and EUR for covered euro-area venues, without currency conversion. In the new European menu dataset, source checks are distinguished from customer observations. Older Italian records retain their existing reporting-date format; those dates do not establish a customer payment. Missing prices remain unavailable rather than being estimated. The core consumer features are the same across supported regions.

6. Business model and regulated services
Consumer features are free. Any venue sponsorship or verified-manager agreement is arranged outside the app. Sponsored placement is labelled and does not purchase customer prices or change cheapest-price comparisons. The app provides information about venues and drinks, not regulated financial, medical or gambling services, alcohol ordering, or fulfilment. Map attribution is provided in the app. Operator: Roberto Valeri; contact: dovebevo@gmail.com.
