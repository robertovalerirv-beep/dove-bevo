1. Purpose and audience
Dove Bevo helps adults 18+ find drinking venues, compare reported or source-checked drink prices and serving sizes, save exact drinks at venues privately, and see aggregate recommendations. It does not sell, order, deliver or take payment for alcohol.

2. Access and main flows
No account, login, demo credentials or account deletion is needed. On first launch, read Rules and Privacy and confirm age 18+. Location is optional: deny it, tap Mappa, then use the map's lower-left search button or “Avvicinati o cerca città” to find Milano and open a venue. The location control is bottom-centre beside Aggiungi prezzo. Bottom tabs are Home, Mappa, Preferiti and Consigliati. Home opens the native Scopri price list. Menu prices are distinguished from customer reports. Tap Save beside one venue's drink to add it to private Preferiti. Its adjacent thumb toggles one public recommendation for that exact drink and shows the total. Consigliati only displays recommended venue/drink pairs and totals for the selected area. The top three-dot menu provides Share, Reload, Privacy and app information where available. Price entry, venue proposal and report forms can be inspected and cancelled without posting. Maps and current prices require internet.

3. Contributions and safety
Users can submit numeric prices, confirmations, missing-drink names, venue proposals and one recommendation per exact drink. New names and venues go to operator review. Visible price and venue report controls cover incorrect or offensive content; operators can hide or remove entries. Reports do not promise immediate removal. There are no public author profiles, direct messages, reviews or photo uploads. There is no consumer control to block a contributor. Content complaints: dovebevo@gmail.com.

4. External services
The first-party HTTPS app and API at www.dovebevo.com run on Cloudflare; Turnstile protects contribution sessions. OpenFreeMap supplies tiles and OpenStreetMap supplies attributed venue/map data. Search uses first-party catalogue and approved venues, not external geocoding. Source-checked prices link to the venue menu and show a check date. Directions open Apple Maps on request; sharing and email use system interfaces. No live AI, in-app payment, native advertising or tracking SDK.

5. Regional differences
Coverage varies by city in Italy and selected European cities. Prices retain their serving and original currency, including GBP in the UK and EUR in euro-area venues, without silent conversion. Missing prices are not estimated. Main controls work in all regions; available venues and exact Save/recommendation controls depend on published data. Milano demonstrates these controls.

6. Business model and material
Consumer features are free; there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Sponsorship or manager arrangements are outside the app and cannot buy a customer price. This is an adult information directory, not an alcohol retailer or a regulated medical, financial or gambling service. OpenStreetMap attribution and links to originating menus are shown; no full third-party menu artwork is presented as ours. Operator: Roberto Valeri. Support: dovebevo@gmail.com.
