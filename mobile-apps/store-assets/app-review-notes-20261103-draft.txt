1. Purpose and audience
Dove Bevo helps adults 18+ find drinking venues and compare drink prices. It distinguishes customer reports from prices checked against published venue menus, with source and check date. People can privately save an exact drink at a venue and see aggregate recommendations. The app does not sell, order, deliver or take payment for alcohol.

2. Access and main flows
No account, login, demo credentials or account deletion is needed. On first launch, read Rules and Privacy and confirm age 18+. Location is optional: deny it, open Mappa, use map search or “Avvicinati o cerca città”, select Milano, choose a drink from the dropdown and open a venue. Locate sits beside Aggiungi prezzo at the map bottom. Bottom tabs: Home, Mappa, Preferiti, Consigliati. Home opens native Scopri for area price comparisons. In a venue, tap Salva beside a drink to save that exact venue/drink pair privately in Preferiti. Its thumb adds/removes a public recommendation and shows the total. Consigliati lists recommended venue/drink pairs for the selected area; votes are made beside drinks. The three-dot menu has available Share, Reload, Privacy and information actions. Price-entry, venue-proposal and report forms can be inspected and cancelled without posting. Maps and live prices need internet.

3. Contributions and safety
Visitors can submit structured prices, confirm/report prices, suggest missing drinks or venues, and recommend exact drinks. New names and venues need operator review before publication. Price/venue reports cover incorrect or offensive content; the operator can hide/remove entries. Reports do not promise immediate removal. There are no public author profiles, direct messages, comments, text reviews, photo uploads or consumer blocking control; contributors cannot contact one another. Content complaints: dovebevo@gmail.com.

4. External services
The first-party HTTPS app/API at www.dovebevo.com run on Cloudflare; Turnstile protects contributions. OpenFreeMap provides tiles and OpenStreetMap attributed map/venue data. Search uses our catalogue/approved venues. Source-checked prices link to the venue menu; a menu check is not a customer payment. Directions opens Apple Maps on request; Share/email use iOS interfaces. No live AI, account provider, payment processor, in-app purchase, or third-party tracking/advertising SDK.

5. Regional differences
Coverage varies across Italian and selected European cities. Prices retain original currency/serving: GBP in UK venues, EUR in euro-area venues, with no silent conversion. Missing prices are not estimated. Main controls work everywhere; venues, prices and vote totals vary with published data. Milano demonstrates the flows.

6. Business model, regulated content and rights
Consumer features are free, with no subscription or paid content. Venue sponsorship/verified-manager agreements are arranged outside the app. Paid venue placement in a first-party list is labelled “Sponsorizzato” with the manager badge; payment cannot buy a customer price or change cheapest-price comparisons. This is an adult directory, not an alcohol retailer/delivery service or a medical, financial or gambling service. Map attribution is visible. Drink art is illustrative; menu sources are linked, and full third-party menu artwork is not presented as ours. Operator: Roberto Valeri. Support: dovebevo@gmail.com.
