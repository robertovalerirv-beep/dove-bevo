1. Purpose and audience
Dove Bevo helps adults aged 18 or older find drinking venues, compare reported or source-checked drink prices and serving sizes, privately save a drink at a venue, and see aggregate drink recommendations. It does not sell, order, deliver or take payment for alcohol.

2. Access and typical flow
No account, login, demo credentials or account-deletion flow is needed. First read Rules and Privacy and confirm age 18+. Location is optional: deny it, tap Mappa, use lower-left Search or “Avvicinati o cerca città” to choose Milano, select a drink from the dropdown and open a venue. The map's Home icon resets the city view; Locate requests device location; Aggiungi prezzo opens contribution choices. The four bottom tabs are Home, Mappa, Preferiti and Consigliati. Home opens Scopri, sorting comparable prices low to high. A venue separates owner-menu prices from customer observations and shows source, serving and fee caveats. Tap Salva beside a drink to keep it privately on this device; tap its adjacent thumb to recommend it publicly and see the total. Preferiti compares saved drink/venue pairs; Consigliati lists recommendations and totals for the selected area, without a vote form. The top three-dot menu offers Share, Reload, Privacy and app information where available. Price-entry, missing-venue and reporting forms can be opened and cancelled without posting. Maps and current prices need internet.

3. Contributions and safety
Customers can submit numeric prices and confirmations, propose a missing drink name or venue, and recommend a drink. New names and venues enter operator review; pending venues are not normal map results. Price and venue reports cover incorrect or offensive content; the operator can review, hide or remove entries. Reports do not promise instant removal. There are no public author profiles, direct messages, reviews or photo uploads. Users cannot contact or follow each other, so no contributor-blocking control is offered. Content complaints: dovebevo@gmail.com.

4. External services
The first-party HTTPS service at www.dovebevo.com runs on Cloudflare with its API/database; Turnstile protects contributions. OpenFreeMap supplies tiles and OpenStreetMap supplies attributed venue/map data. Search uses first-party catalogues and approved venues, not external geocoding. Menu prices link to their source and show a check date. Directions open Apple Maps on request; sharing and email use system interfaces. There is no live AI, in-app payment, native advertising or tracking SDK.

5. Regional differences
Coverage varies across Italy and selected European cities. Prices keep their original serving and currency, including GBP in the UK and EUR in covered euro-area venues, without silent conversion. Missing prices are not estimated. Main controls are the same in supported regions; venue and price availability varies by area. Milano demonstrates the main flows.

6. Business model and material
Consumer features are free; there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Sponsorship or manager arrangements are outside the app and cannot buy a customer price or change cheapest-price comparisons. This adult information directory is not an alcohol retailer or a regulated medical, financial or gambling service. OpenStreetMap attribution and links to venue menus are shown; no full third-party menu artwork is presented as ours. Operator: Roberto Valeri. Support: dovebevo@gmail.com.
