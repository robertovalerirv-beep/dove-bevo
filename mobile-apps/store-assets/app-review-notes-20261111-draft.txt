1. Purpose and audience
Dove Bevo helps adults aged 18 or older find drinking venues, compare reported or source-checked drink prices and serving sizes, privately save a drink at a venue, and see aggregate drink recommendations. It does not sell, order, deliver or take payment for alcohol.

2. Access and typical flow
No login, demo credentials or account-deletion flow. First read Rules/Privacy and confirm age 18+. Location is optional: deny it, tap Mappa, choose Milano with lower-left Search or “Avvicinati o cerca città”, select a drink from the dropdown and open a venue. Map Home resets the city view; Locate requests location; Aggiungi prezzo opens contribution choices. Bottom tabs: Home, Mappa, Preferiti, Consigliati. Home/Scopri sorts comparable prices low to high. Venue details separate owner-menu prices from customer observations and show source, serving and fee caveats. Salva beside a drink stores it privately on this device; its adjacent thumb recommends it publicly and shows the total. Preferiti compares saved drink/venue pairs; Consigliati lists area recommendations and totals, without a vote form. The top three-dot menu offers Share, Reload, Privacy and app information where available. Price-entry, missing-venue and reporting forms can be cancelled without posting. Maps and current prices need internet.

3. Contributions and safety
Customers can submit numeric prices, confirmations, missing drink/venue proposals and recommendations. New names and venues enter operator review; pending venues are not normal map results. Price and venue reports cover incorrect or offensive content; the operator may hide or remove entries, not necessarily immediately. There are no public author profiles, direct messages, reviews or photo uploads. Users cannot contact or follow each other, so no contributor-blocking control exists. Content complaints: dovebevo@gmail.com.

4. External services
The first-party HTTPS service at www.dovebevo.com runs on Cloudflare with its API/database; Turnstile protects contributions. OpenFreeMap supplies tiles and OpenStreetMap supplies attributed venue/map data. Search uses first-party catalogues and approved venues, not external geocoding. Menu prices link to their source and show a check date. Directions open Apple Maps on request; sharing and email use system interfaces. There is no live AI, in-app payment or third-party advertising/tracking SDK. Clearly labelled sponsored venue placement may appear; aggregate daily venue-view counts are recorded without linking them to people.

5. Regional differences
Coverage varies across Italy and selected European cities. Prices keep their original serving and currency, including GBP in the UK and EUR in covered euro-area venues, without silent conversion. Missing prices are not estimated. Main controls are the same in supported regions; venue and price availability varies by area. Milano demonstrates the main flows.

6. Business model and material
Consumer features are free; no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Paid verified venues may appear first in the app venue list with a “Sponsorizzato” label; contracts and payment are external. Placement does not alter drink prices, the map or Scopri's cheapest-price comparison. This adult information directory is not an alcohol retailer or a medical, financial or gambling service. OpenStreetMap attribution and venue-menu links are shown; no full third-party menu artwork is presented as ours. Operator: Roberto Valeri. Support: dovebevo@gmail.com.
